How to write in

Write-in candidates aren't printed on the ballot, so you add the name yourself. On your Washington ballot:

  1. 1

    Find the race

    Locate U.S. Representative, Congressional District 7.

  2. 2

    Write the name

    On the blank write-in line, clearly print Peter Absolom Mbatia.

  3. 3

    Mark it

    Fill in the oval next to the write-in line, as your ballot's instructions show.

  4. 4

    Return it by Nov 3

    Put your ballot in a drop box by 8 p.m. or mail it postmarked by Election Day, November 3, 2026.

Washington write-in rules

  • A write-in candidate must file a Declaration of Write-in Candidacy by 8 p.m. on Election Day for their votes to be counted (RCW 29A.24.311).
  • Write-in candidates in the general election don't appear on the ballot, on the state website, or in the Voters' Pamphlet.
  • Write-in votes for declared candidates are only tallied by name if the total write-in vote in a race is high enough to change the outcome (RCW 29A.60.021).
